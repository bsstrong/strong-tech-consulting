# Alabama Local Occupational-Tax Verification — August 27, 2026

## Outcome

All 20 Alabama League association-supported rows were researched against current municipal, ordinance, or delegated-collector material. Nine rows now have sufficient primary evidence for current employee-compensation incidence, worksite scope, rate, and employer withholding and are promoted to `CONFIRMED_PRIMARY`. Eleven remain `SUPPORTED_AUTHORITATIVE_ASSOCIATION` because at least the worksite/nonresident rule could not be closed.

This work verifies known candidates; it does not close Alabama's statewide adopter universe. Alabama therefore remains `PARTIAL`.
