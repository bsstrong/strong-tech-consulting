# Idaho closure audit - August 26, 2026

## Disposition

`NO_AUTHORITY_CONFIRMED`; zero registry rows.

Idaho Constitution article VII, section 6 requires legislative investment of municipal taxing power. *North Idaho Building Contractors Association v. City of Hayden*, 156 Idaho 721, 329 P.3d 466 (2015), holds that Idaho Code section 50-301 is not a general taxing grant and that municipal taxes on the public require specific legislative authorization. Constitution article III, section 19 prohibits local or special laws for tax assessment and collection.

The closure audit retrieved all 74 title indexes and 1,471 linked chapter PDFs from the current Idaho Code. There were 1,470 valid PDFs; the one dead chapter link was recovered through the live chapter and all section pages. Exact and proximity searches across 37.2 MB of normalized statutory text found no grant for a municipal percentage tax measured by employee wages, earnings, compensation, or payroll. Every candidate context combining municipal and income/compensation-tax terms was reviewed.

The actual resort-city delegation in Idaho Code sections 50-1044 through 50-1049 is limited to short-term lodging occupancy, drink, and sales taxes. The current State Tax Commission city-tax page corroborates that topology.
