# Wyoming source extracts

## Wyoming Statutes section 39-12-101

Wyoming preempts the field of income taxes, earnings taxes, and every other tax based on wages or other income. The section expressly states that no county, city, town, or other political subdivision has the right to impose, levy, or collect those taxes.

This provision directly covers the national registry definition and closes both general municipal and home-rule theories.

Source: Title 39 PDF and extracted text within `Wyoming-current-code-titles.zip`.

## Wyoming Constitution article 13, section 3

The Legislature must restrict municipal powers to tax and assess, and no municipal tax or assessment may be levied or collected except pursuant to law for a public purpose specified by law.

Source: Constitution Title 97 PDF and extracted text within `Wyoming-current-code-titles.zip`.

## Wyoming Constitution article 15, section 13

No tax may be levied except pursuant to law, and the law imposing it must distinctly state its object.
