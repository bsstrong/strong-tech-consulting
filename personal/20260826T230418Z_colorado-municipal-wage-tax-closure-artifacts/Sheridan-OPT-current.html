Occupational Privilege Tax

Description & Amount

A $3 per month occupational privilege tax is due from each employee through payroll withholding. A $3 per month match is due from the employer for each employee. The employer is responsible for remitting both the employee and employer portions of the tax to the City of Sheridan.

The Sheridan Occupational Privilege Tax is a tax on employers and employees, not Sheridan residents.

Remittance Submissions

Remittance of Sheridan Occupational Privilege Tax should be submitted to: City of Sheridan via MuniRevs

For More Information

For more information about Sheridan’s occupational privilege tax, please contact the Finance Department at 303-762-2200.
