# Phase 3 Colorado closure

- Research date: 2026-08-26
- Prior status: `UNDETERMINED`
- Final status: `NO_AUTHORITY_CONFIRMED`
- Registry rows added: 0

Current Colorado Constitution article X, section 20(8)(a) expressly prohibits local district income taxes, and section 20(2)(b) defines district to include local government. The current Colorado Legislative Drafting Manual confirms the prohibition applies to any local-government income tax.

*City & County of Denver v. Sweet* held that article X, section 17 gives the General Assembly exclusive, nondelegable income-tax power and leaves even home-rule cities without authority to impose income taxes. *Duffy* reaffirmed that rule and invalidated Denver's percentage earnings tax. *Johnson* distinguishes valid flat occupational fees because they are not measured by income. *Rountree* preserved that state-law distinction.

Current Glendale, Greenwood Village, Sheridan, and Denver employee occupational privilege taxes are fixed monthly amounts. They remain excluded because the registry requires a percentage or graduated tax calculated from employee compensation.

Refresh after a constitutional amendment, a controlling decision changing the Colorado-law classification, or a new authority that survives the constitutional limits.
