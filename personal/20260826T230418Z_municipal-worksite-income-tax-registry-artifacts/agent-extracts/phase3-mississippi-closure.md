# Mississippi closure — August 26, 2026

**Disposition:** `NO_AUTHORITY_CONFIRMED`; zero registry rows.

Mississippi Code section 21-17-5(2) requires a specific statute or law before a municipality may levy any tax. The current codified municipal taxation framework, the State Auditor's mandatory all-municipality accounting/compliance guide, current Department of Revenue withholding and special-local-levy systems, and an official 1997-2026 regular-session audit reveal no enacted municipal percentage tax on employee wages, earnings, compensation, or payroll.

The Legislature introduced the exact missing authority five times—1997 HB 68, 1998 HB 299, 1999 HB 950, 2001 HB 900, and 2003 HB 1467. Each died in House committee. The proposed measures would have covered income from work in an eligible city, allocated nonresident work, and used state administration; none became law.

The Job Development Assessment Fee sometimes reported in the W-2 local box is excluded. It services Mississippi Business Finance Corporation project bonds and is not imposed by a municipality.
