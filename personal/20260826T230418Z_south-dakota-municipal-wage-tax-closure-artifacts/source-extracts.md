# South Dakota source extracts

## Constitution article IX, section 2

A chartered governmental unit may exercise legislative powers not denied by its charter, the Constitution, or general state law, and home-rule powers are construed liberally. This prevents a categorical no-authority conclusion without a more specific prohibition.

Source: `South-Dakota-Constitution-current.html`.

## SDCL 6-12-14

No county, city, or other governmental unit, including a home-rule unit, may enact or increase a tax, fee, or charge similar to a tax producing state revenue unless statute specifically provides otherwise. Pre-March 1, 1996 taxes and fees are grandfathered.

Source: `SDCL-6-12-current.html`.

## SDCL 10-52-1 and 10-52-2

Chapter 10-52 defines a non-ad-valorem tax as any tax other than an ad-valorem real-property tax and authorizes an incorporated municipality to impose a non-ad-valorem tax in accordance with the chapter. The breadth of this language is why the final classification is a current `COMPLETE` zero rather than `NO_AUTHORITY_CONFIRMED`.

Source: `SDCL-10-52-current.html`.

## SDCL 10-52-4 and 10-52-9

DOR administers a chapter 10-52 municipal tax whenever the state imposes a similar tax and may contract to administer other taxes. Every new or amended chapter 10-52 tax ordinance must be delivered to the Secretary of Revenue at least 90 days before its January 1 or July 1 effective date.

Source: `SDCL-10-52-current.html`.

## DOR July 2026 Municipal Tax Guide

The guide states that SDCL chapters 10-52 and 10-52A allow municipalities to impose municipal sales/use and gross-receipts taxes and identifies DOR as the administrative and enforcement agency. Its current city-code tables identify no income, wage, earnings, compensation, or payroll tax category or adopter.

Source: `SD-DOR-Municipal-Tax-Guide-July-2026.pdf`, pages 3 and 6-10; extracted text in the adjacent `.txt` file.

## SDCL 10-64-1

The Legislature states that South Dakota has no income tax. Chapter 10-43 separately taxes financial institutions; section 10-43-5 makes that tax in lieu of nearly all local taxes on those institutions and does not authorize a municipal employee tax.

Source: title 10 within `South-Dakota-current-code-titles.zip` and `SDCL-10-43-current.html`.
