Current Wyoming Statutes section 39-12-101 expressly preempts income taxes, earnings taxes, and every other tax based on wages or other income. It denies every county, city, town, and other political subdivision the right to impose, levy, or collect those taxes. Wyoming Constitution article 13, section 3 and article 15, section 13 also require municipal and other taxes to rest on law.

The complete current-code cross-check downloaded statutory Titles 1 through 42 and Constitution Title 97. The 43-file, 12,429-page corpus contains no qualifying exception, delegation, or adopter. The two `local income tax` hits and four proximity hits are unrelated recordkeeping, FICA, deferred-compensation, economic-development, and pension provisions.

Refresh after amendment or repeal of section 39-12-101, a constitutional change affecting municipal taxing power, or an express statutory exception for a local employee wage- or income-based tax.
