# Montana closure source extracts

## Current legal restriction and grant

[MCA section 7-1-112 (2025)](https://mca.legmt.gov/bills/mca/title_0070/chapter_0010/part_0010/section_0120/0070-0010-0010-0120.html) says a self-government local government is prohibited from exercising “the power to authorize a tax on income” unless specifically delegated by law.

[MCA section 7-6-4401 (2025)](https://mca.legmt.gov/bills/mca/title_0070/chapter_0060/part_0440/section_0010/0070-0060-0440-0010.html) authorizes city and town councils to levy general and special taxes on property within the municipality. [Section 7-6-4421](https://mca.legmt.gov/bills/mca/title_0070/chapter_0060/part_0440/section_0210/0070-0060-0440-0210.html) authorizes annual municipal taxes on property taxable for state and county purposes.

## Adopter statement

The [Montana Department of Revenue's 2023 Form 2 instructions](https://mtrevenue.gov/wp-content/uploads/dlm_uploads/2023/12/Form_2_2023_Instructions.pdf) state: “No city in Montana imposes a local income tax.” The official 2019, 2020, 2021, and 2022 Form 2 instructions contain the same statement. The 2024 form redesign discontinued the Montana itemized-deduction schedule that had prompted this instruction.

## Post-2023 current-law check

The Legislature's July 2025 [Summary of Enacted Tax Legislation](https://archive.legmt.gov/content/Committees/Interim/2025-2026/RIC/Meetings/July_11_2025/3.3.tax-legislation-2025.pdf) inventories enacted individual/corporate income, miscellaneous, property, and tax-administration legislation. Its complete text contains no `local income`, `municipal income`, `city income`, `wage tax`, `earnings tax`, or `payroll tax` delegation.

The [2023 Legislature/MSU Local Government Center presentation](https://archive.legmt.gov/content/Committees/Interim/2023-2024/Local-Government/Meetings/September-7-2023/2.01_VoterReview_MSU.LGCenter_9.4.23.pdf) places the power to authorize an income tax under “Power Denied or Requiring Delegation” and cites sections 7-1-111 and 7-1-112.
