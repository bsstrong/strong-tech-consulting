# Utah current-code manual hit dispositions

Every nonzero exact-phrase result and every municipal/employee/compensation proximity candidate was reviewed. None is a municipal employee wage-tax grant or adopter.

## `payroll tax` — 3 hits

All three occur in Title 58 professional-licensing provisions. They refer to compliance with federal and state employee income-tax withholding, unemployment tax, Social Security payroll tax, or similar required withholding. They do not authorize any Utah local tax.

Disposition: unrelated federal/state payroll-compliance references.

## `local income tax` — 10 hits

- Title 35A: generic notice that unemployment benefits may be subject to federal, state, and local income tax.
- Title 53H: bond interest is not subject to state or local income taxation.
- Title 59: federal-itemized-deduction definitions referring generically to state or local income tax paid by a claimant, estate, or trust.

Disposition: generic multijurisdictional recordkeeping, benefit-notice, bond-exemption, and federal-deduction language—not Utah municipal authority.
