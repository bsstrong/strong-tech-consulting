# Montana municipal worksite wage-tax closure

## Outcome

**Disposition: `NO_AUTHORITY_CONFIRMED`; zero registry rows.**

As of August 26, 2026, no Montana municipality has current authority or a current levy matching this registry's percentage-based, employer-withheld employee worksite wage-tax definition. Montana therefore moves from `UNDETERMINED` to a closed zero. The national registry remains 2,815 municipality rows across nine states.

This iteration took approximately 12 minutes, within the one-hour state cap.

## Definition applied

The registry includes only a municipality or city-like government's percentage tax calculated from an employee's wages, earnings, compensation, or payroll because the employee works there, including a nonresident commuter, with employer-withholding relevance.

It excludes resident-only income taxes, employer-only payroll-expense taxes, flat occupational or head taxes, sales/property/business taxes, county or district taxes, and taxes limited to business profits or self-employment.

## Why Montana is closed

1. **Local income-tax power requires a specific legislative delegation.** Current [Montana Code Annotated section 7-1-112](https://mca.legmt.gov/bills/mca/title_0070/chapter_0010/part_0010/section_0120/0070-0010-0010-0120.html) prohibits a self-government local government from exercising the power to authorize a tax on income unless that power is specifically delegated by law. A 2023 Legislative Services/MSU Local Government Center presentation independently identifies the power to authorize an income tax as denied or requiring delegation.

2. **The current general municipal-tax grant is property-only.** Current [section 7-6-4401](https://mca.legmt.gov/bills/mca/title_0070/chapter_0060/part_0440/section_0010/0070-0060-0440-0010.html) authorizes city and town councils to levy taxes on property within the municipality. [Section 7-6-4421](https://mca.legmt.gov/bills/mca/title_0070/chapter_0060/part_0440/section_0210/0070-0060-0440-0210.html) likewise authorizes annual municipal taxes on property taxable for state and county purposes. Neither supplies the specific income-tax delegation required by section 7-1-112.

3. **The Department of Revenue closes the adopter universe at zero.** Montana Department of Revenue Form 2 instructions for tax year 2023 expressly state that no Montana city imposes a local income tax. The same statement appears in the official 2019, 2020, 2021, and 2022 instructions. The 2024 form redesign eliminated the itemized-deduction line that prompted the statement; it does not identify a new local tax.

4. **The 2025 Legislature did not create the missing authority.** The Legislature's July 2025 [Summary of Enacted Tax Legislation](https://archive.legmt.gov/content/Committees/Interim/2025-2026/RIC/Meetings/July_11_2025/3.3.tax-legislation-2025.pdf) accounts for the enacted individual/corporate income, miscellaneous, property, and tax-administration measures and contains no local or municipal income-tax delegation. The post-session 2025 MCA still presents section 7-1-112 unchanged and the municipal general-tax provisions as property taxes.

5. **Current-code searches produced no alternate wage-tax grant.** Searches of the current 2025 MCA for local/municipal/city income tax and municipal taxes measured by wages, earnings, compensation, or payroll produced only generic multistate references, recordkeeping language, and unrelated employment taxes. No specific delegation to a city or town was found.

The result is stronger than an absence from a common-tax webpage: it combines the current delegation restriction, the actual municipal taxing grant, an authoritative no-adopter statement, and a post-2025-session enacted-law check.

## Caveat and refresh rule

`NO_AUTHORITY_CONFIRMED` is a current legal conclusion, not a promise that Montana law cannot change. Refresh after any amendment to sections 7-1-112, 7-6-4401, or 7-6-4421; any new specific local income-tax delegation; or Department of Revenue material identifying a city income, earnings, wage, compensation, or payroll tax.
