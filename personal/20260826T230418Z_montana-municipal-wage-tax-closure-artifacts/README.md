# Montana municipal worksite wage-tax closure evidence

Retrieved August 26, 2026. This evidence set supports the conclusion in the [Montana closure report](../20260826T230418Z_montana-municipal-wage-tax-closure.md).

## Method and result

- Applied current MCA section 7-1-112's specific-delegation requirement for a local tax on income.
- Reviewed current sections 7-6-4401 and 7-6-4421, which make the general municipal taxing grant property-based.
- Confirmed the Legislature/MSU Local Government Center's official explanation that income-tax authority is denied or requires delegation.
- Used Montana DOR's tax-year 2023 instruction that no Montana city imposes a local income tax as the adopter-universe check; the same statement recurred in the 2019-2022 instructions.
- Searched the complete July 2025 enacted-tax-legislation summary and the post-session current MCA for a new or alternate municipal income/wage-tax delegation. None was found.

## Files

- `MCA-2025-7-1-112.html` — current income-tax delegation restriction.
- `MCA-2025-7-6-4401.html` and `MCA-2025-7-6-4421.html` — current municipal property-tax grants.
- `Montana-Local-Government-Powers-2023.pdf` and `.txt` — Legislature/MSU Local Government Center explanation of denied/delegated powers.
- `Montana-2025-Enacted-Tax-Legislation-Summary.pdf` and `.txt` — complete 2025 enacted-tax measure summary and searchable extraction.
- `montana-search-audit.json` — current-code and current-session search scope and dispositions.
- `source-extracts.md` — decisive source language and the DOR no-adopter statement with URLs.
- `artifact-manifest.csv` — byte count and SHA-256 hash for every other evidence file.
