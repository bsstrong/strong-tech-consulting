# Mississippi municipal worksite wage-tax closure evidence

Retrieved August 26, 2026. This evidence set supports the conclusion in the [Mississippi closure report](../20260826T230418Z_mississippi-municipal-wage-tax-closure.md).

## Method

1. Applied Mississippi Code section 21-17-5(2), which requires a specific statute or law before a municipality may levy any tax.
2. Reviewed the current municipal taxation/finance framework in Title 21, chapter 33.
3. Searched the State Auditor's mandatory municipal accounting and compliance guide for every employee-income/wage/earnings/payroll tax concept and reviewed its complete municipal tax-receipt and audit-procedure classifications.
4. Retrieved and searched the official “All Measures” report for every regular legislative session from 1997 through 2026. The 30-row audit records every source URL, byte count, SHA-256 hash, exact-phrase result, and qualifying candidate.
5. Reviewed all five exact local-income-tax proposals and their official histories. Each died in House committee.
6. Cross-checked current Department of Revenue withholding, city/local levy, legislation, and January 2026 special-levy distribution material.
7. Kept the Mississippi Business Finance Corporation Job Development Assessment Fee excluded because it is a project-bond assessment, not a municipal levy.

## Legislative result

| Session | Measure | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1997 | HB 68 | Died in House committee, February 26, 1997 |
| 1998 | HB 299 | Died in House committee, February 25, 1998 |
| 1999 | HB 950 | Died in House committee, February 24, 1999 |
| 2001 | HB 900 | Died in House committee, February 21, 2001 |
| 2003 | HB 1467 | Died in House committee, February 26, 2003 |

No other 1997-2026 regular-session “All Measures” report contains the exact phrase `local income tax`. Broader searches for municipal income, tax on wages, earnings tax, and payroll tax produced no enacted qualifying authority; unrelated refund-setoff and generic references to taxes in other jurisdictions were rejected.
