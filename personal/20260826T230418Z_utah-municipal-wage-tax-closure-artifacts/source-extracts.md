# Utah source extracts

## Utah Constitution article XI, section 5

A charter city may levy, assess, and collect taxes only within limits prescribed by general law. The general municipal-affairs grant therefore does not create an unrestricted local taxing power.

Source: `Utah-Constitution-Article-XI-current.pdf`.

## *Moss v. Board of Commissioners of Salt Lake City* (Utah 1953)

The Utah Supreme Court held that municipal taxing power is derived solely from legislative enactment, reaches only authority expressly conferred or necessarily implied, and may not be inferred from convenience or ambiguous law. Doubt about tax authority is resolved in favor of the taxpayer.

Source: [Justia opinion mirror](https://law.justia.com/cases/utah/supreme-court/1953/8092-0.html), citing 1 Utah 2d 60, 261 P.2d 961.

## Utah Code section 10-1-203

Subsection (1)(a) defines `Business` for the municipal license-fee and tax section and expressly excludes employees rendering services to employers. Subsection (2) authorizes business regulation and cost-recovery fees. Subsections (3) through (5) enumerate municipal revenue authority for energy sales/use, telecommunications, parking-service businesses, public-assembly tickets, and specified disproportionate-cost or enhanced-service businesses. They do not authorize a tax calculated from an employee's wages or compensation.

Source: Title 10 PDF and extracted text within `Utah-current-code-titles.zip`.

## Utah Code section 59-10-402

Subsection (3) requires each employer paying wages to withhold an amount designed to pay the income tax imposed by Utah Code Title 59, chapter 10. The provision is a state individual-income-tax withholding rule; it does not create or administer municipal withholding.

Source: Title 59 PDF and extracted text within `Utah-current-code-titles.zip`.

## April 2026 Utah Withholding Tax Guide

Publication 14 describes registration, filing, payment, schedules, and tables for Utah withholding tax calculated from employee wages. It identifies no municipal income, wage, earnings, compensation, or payroll withholding system. This is corroborating administrative evidence; the no-authority conclusion rests principally on article XI, *Moss*, section 10-1-203, and the complete current-code audit.

Source: `Utah-Publication-14-April-2026.pdf` and extracted `.txt`.
