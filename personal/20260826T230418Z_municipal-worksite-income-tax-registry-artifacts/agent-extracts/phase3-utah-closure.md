Utah Constitution article XI, section 5 limits charter-city taxing power to the bounds prescribed by general law. *Moss v. Board of Commissioners of Salt Lake City*, 1 Utah 2d 60, 261 P.2d 961 (1953), holds that a municipality has only taxing authority expressly conferred or necessarily implied by legislative enactment and that doubtful authority is resolved for the taxpayer.

Current section 10-1-203 expressly excludes employees rendering services to employers from the `Business` definition used by its municipal license-fee and tax authority. Its revenue provisions enumerate energy, telecommunications, parking, public-assembly, and specified service-cost taxes, not employee wages. Section 59-10-402 and the April 2026 withholding guide administer only the Utah state income tax.

The complete current-code audit selected and downloaded all 96 official current title PDFs. The 19,339-page, 37,553,810-PDF-byte corpus contains no qualifying delegation or adopter. Every nonzero `payroll tax`, `local income tax`, and municipal/compensation proximity hit was an unrelated federal/state withholding, recordkeeping, bond, deduction, consumer, wage-payment, sewage, or FICA provision.

Refresh on amendment to article XI, section 5; section 10-1-203; Title 59 chapter 10; a new general-law municipal employee-compensation tax grant; or controlling case law changing the *Moss* rule.
